River Waste Collection Boat: live simulation

ESP32 catamaran with conveyor belt, ultrasonic waste detection and IR bin-full sensing. Same logic and pin numbers as the real firmware.

Drive with the buttons or the arrow keys / WASD (Space = stop). Press Auto-collect, then steer the front of the boat toward floating waste: when the HC-SR04 reads under 20 cm three times in a row, the boat stops and the conveyor runs for 10 s.
